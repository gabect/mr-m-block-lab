# Block Lab — Educational Use Notice
**Last updated:** October 27, 2026

Block Lab is an independent teaching tool for introductory algorithms, sequencing, testing, and debugging. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Code.org or Google.

- **Classroom practice:** Mission completion messages are learning feedback, not an official grade or school submission.
- **Evidence:** Students may copy or download a mission screenshot and submit it through a teacher-approved system such as Google Slides. Capturing a screenshot does not automatically turn in an assignment.
- **Availability:** The application is provided for instructional use. Browsers, school device settings, clipboard permissions, or storage policies may affect features.
- **Storage:** Progress is stored only in the browser and may be lost if local data is cleared. Do not rely on it as the sole record of assessed work.
- **Accessibility:** Teachers should provide equivalent alternatives when drag-and-drop, keyboard, visual, or clipboard interactions are difficult.
- **Privacy:** Read [Privacy & Data Notice](PRIVACY.md) before classroom use, especially on shared devices.

This is a practical educational notice, not a substitute for review by the school or its legal/privacy team.
